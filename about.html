About us

Not a corporate story. Just a father who started something, and a son trying to scale and do it justice.

Where it began

More than fifty years ago, Papa started this business from scratch. Early mornings, a good eye for produce, and a simple belief that people deserve the best quality, smooth delivery, and service they can count on.

Trust didn’t come overnight. Papa showed up, stayed honest even on the off days, and slowly people started trusting us with their kitchens. We’re lucky our customers have stood by us for years, and that means more than we can say.

Papa didn’t leave us a big plan. He left us a name people trust.

Who looks after it now

Papa is no longer with us. Today I look after the business along with a small team of loyal staff who believe in what we do and choose to stay with us.

Same roots, bigger dreams: keeping the quality Papa believed in, and growing it with the people who made it possible.

What we care about

Freshness first. Fair dealing. And showing up in the neighbourhoods we serve like Matunga to Worli, Dadar to Lower Parel, and the stretches in between.

If you’ve been with us for years, thank you. If you’re new, welcome. Either way, we’re glad you’re here.
